# Diagnosis playbook

Use this only after deterministic, cohort-aware triage.

## Q1 — Search High / AI High

Goal: protect what already works.

Check:

1. Which answer modules are likely carrying the page?
2. Which first-party evidence is unique?
3. What is stale or at risk of decay?
4. Are there adjacent decision questions that belong on the same page?
5. Is the page strong within the correct peer cohort, not just sitewide?

Typical actions:

- PROTECT
- REFRESH
- DEEPEN

Avoid full rewrites without evidence of decline, task mismatch, or structural failure.

## Q2 — Search High / AI Low

Goal: determine whether there is a real AI-search gap.

Sequence:

1. Confirm the Q2 classification is relative to an appropriate cohort.
2. Validate AI-answer opportunity for representative tasks.
3. Identify the Decision Task.
4. Compare task vs page promise.
5. Identify missing answer assets.
6. Identify missing proof/evidence.
7. Check delivery/eligibility issues.
8. Choose action.

Interpretation examples:

- AI rarely appears for the task → MONITOR / PROTECT
- AI answers "A vs B" but page answers "What is A?" → CREATE or REBUILD
- Page answers the task but lacks comparison criteria → DEEPEN
- Page answers the task but has only generic claims → add evidence, testing, cases, methodology
- Page is blocked, non-canonical, not snippet-eligible, or key content is unavailable → FIX_DELIVERY

## Q3 — Search Low / AI High

Goal: understand what AI systems may be finding useful that classic Search metrics understate.

Sequence:

1. Confirm the Q3 classification is relative to an appropriate cohort.
2. Identify likely common Decision Task across related queries/questions.
3. Inspect what content module is unusually specific or evidence-rich.
4. Check whether the page is serving a complex comparison, evaluation, or implementation task.
5. Decide whether to deepen the same asset or create a distinct task-based asset.

Avoid:

- one page per long query
- one page per prompt variant
- assuming every AI-high page should be expanded

## Q4 — Search Low / AI Low

Goal: avoid wasting resources.

Ask:

1. Is there real demand?
2. Is there commercial or strategic value?
3. Is there unique evidence or differentiation?
4. Is the page duplicated, outdated, cannibalizing, or orphaned?
5. Is "low" only an artifact of being compared with an unsuitable sitewide baseline?

Typical actions:

- MERGE
- REFRESH
- MONITOR
- DEPRIORITIZE

## Decision Task → Answer → Evidence → Delivery

### Decision Task

What is the user trying to accomplish?

### Answer Gap

What must the answer contain to complete that task, and what is missing?

### Evidence Gap

Why should a user or Google trust/use this page rather than a commodity summary?

### Delivery Gap

Can Google reliably crawl, index, render, understand, and show the relevant content as a supporting link?

Do not skip directly from quadrant to formatting advice.

## Converting diagnosis into work

Every shortlisted URL should end in one owner-ready Action Backlog row.

The backlog is where analysis becomes execution:

- quadrant answers **which pages deserve attention**
- diagnosis answers **why**
- primary action answers **what to do**
- owner/date fields answer **who and when**
- validation plan answers **how we will know**
